# Engineering Calculations & Verification

**Reference Documents:** `ANSYS_Report_Turbine_Blade.pdf` [cite: 1], `GD&T_Turbine_Blade.pdf` [cite: 2]

This document outlines hand calculations to verify the results and inputs generated by the FEA solver.

## 1. Mass and Volume Verification (Identifying the Scale Error)
The FEA report shows a massive discrepancy in the physical scale of the part compared to the manufacturing drawing.

**From GD&T Drawing:**
* Overall approximate diameter $D \approx 256.00 \text{ mm}$ ($0.256 \text{ m}$) [cite: 2].
* Maximum thickness $t = 4.00 \text{ mm}$ ($0.004 \text{ m}$) [cite: 2].

**From ANSYS Report:**
* Bounding Box Length X: $12.256 \text{ m}$ [cite: 1].
* Volume: $224.71 \text{ m}^3$ [cite: 1].
* Mass: $1.0022 \times 10^6 \text{ kg}$ [cite: 1].

**Correction Calculation:**
The ratio of the imported length ($12.256 \text{ m}$) to the physical length ($256 \text{ mm}$) indicates a scale factor error of approximately 1000 (mm imported directly as meters).
* **True Volume** $= 224.71 \text{ m}^3 / (1000)^3 = 224.71 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}^3$.
* **True Mass** $= \text{Density} \times \text{True Volume}$
* **True Mass** $= 4460 \text{ kg/m}^3$ [cite: 1] $\times (224.71 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}^3) = 1.0022 \text{ kg}$.

A mass of $\sim 1 \text{ kg}$ is highly realistic for a $256 \text{ mm}$ titanium impeller.

## 2. Load Verification
* A pressure load ramping up to $1300 \text{ Pa}$ was applied [cite: 1].
* $1300 \text{ Pa} = 0.0013 \text{ MPa}$.
* If this pressure was applied to the incorrectly scaled surface area, the total force $F = P \times A$ is artificially inflated by a factor of $10^6$. The stresses calculated ($14.22 \text{ MPa}$) [cite: 1] must be completely re-evaluated on the corrected geometry.

## 3. Material Yield Check
* **Titanium Alloy Tensile Yield Strength:** $9.3 \times 10^8 \text{ Pa}$ ($930 \text{ MPa}$) [cite: 1].
* **Maximum Equivalent Stress (Current Model):** $1.4225 \times 10^7 \text{ Pa}$ ($14.2 \text{ MPa}$) [cite: 1].
* **Factor of Safety (FoS) on current invalid scale:** $930 / 14.2 \approx 65.4$.
